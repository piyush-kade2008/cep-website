Important Information

Keep the following points in mind while applying for government schemes.

Eligibility

Check the eligibility conditions of the scheme before submitting your application.

Documents

Keep Aadhaar card, bank details, income certificate and other required documents ready.

Application

Apply through the official government portal or visit the concerned Gram Panchayat office.

Verification

Provide correct information and complete verification whenever required.
